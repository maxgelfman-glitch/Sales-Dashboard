# PowerWash CRM

A clean, simple lead-tracking dashboard for a power washing business — built to solve the "oh, I should really reach back out to them" problem. It sits **alongside** Workiz and Go High Level: this is where you track leads, remember follow-ups, and log every conversation, all in one calm, Apple-style interface.

## How to open it

**It's a single file — no installing anything.**

- **Easiest:** double-click `index.html` and it opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge — any of them).
- **Bookmark it** so it's one click away. On a Mac you can even drag the tab to your Dock.
- Works on your phone too — open the file (or the hosted link) and it adapts to the smaller screen.

Your data is saved automatically in that browser on that device. Nothing goes to the cloud, no login, no monthly fee.

> **Back up your data:** click **Export data** in the bottom-left now and then — it downloads a small file with everything in it. **Import data** loads it back (handy for moving to a new computer or phone). The app starts with a handful of sample leads so you can see how it works — just delete them and add your own.

## What it does

**Everything is filtered by Residential vs Commercial** using the toggle at the top — so you can look at just one side of the business or both together.

### 📊 Dashboard
Your morning check-in. Shows active leads, pipeline value, and — front and center — a **"Needs your attention"** list of everyone who's overdue or due for a follow-up this week. This is the antidote to reaching out haphazardly. It also flags active leads that have **no next step scheduled** so nobody slips through the cracks.

### 📋 Pipeline
A drag-and-drop board. Every lead is a card you slide between stages:
**New Lead → Contacted → Quoted → Scheduled → Won / Lost.**
Cards show the job value and a red/amber flag if a follow-up is late or coming up.

### 🔔 Follow-ups
Every lead that needs a touch, grouped by **Overdue / Due today / This week / Upcoming**. The red number badge in the sidebar is your count of overdue items — get it to zero and you're caught up.

### 👥 Contacts
A sortable table of every lead. Click any row to open the full record.

### The lead record (click any lead)
- **Contact info** — phone, email, address are all tappable (call, email, or open in Maps in one tap).
- **Pipeline stage** — change it from a dropdown.
- **Follow-up reminder** — set a "reach back out on ___" date with a note. Quick buttons for *in 2 days / 1 week / 2 weeks / 1 month*.
- **Communication log** — a timeline of every call, text, email, or on-site visit, with the date and what was discussed. When you log a contact it **prompts you to set the next follow-up right then**, so you never leave a lead without a next step.
- **Message templates** — ready-to-go follow-up and win-back messages, personalized with the contact's name. Tap to copy, then paste into a text or email.
- **Notes** — anything else (gate codes, surfaces to clean, pricing).

## Handy shortcuts
- `N` — new lead
- `/` — jump to search
- `Esc` — close any panel

## A note on automation
Because this is a private, no-server tool, it doesn't send texts or emails on its own — instead it makes reaching out *frictionless*: one-tap call/email/map links, copy-ready message templates, and follow-up reminders that surface exactly when it's time. If down the road you want true auto-send (scheduled emails/SMS) or a shared team version with a login, that's a natural next step and would need a small backend — easy to add later on this same foundation.
